🏆 UPI's Unstoppable Volume Dominance
92.22%
UPI captured 92.2% of all digital payment transactions by volume in Apr 2026 — up from 87.7% in Oct 2024. The platform crossed 90% in Dec 2024 and has never dipped below. With 2,23,468 lakh monthly transactions, UPI processes more payments than all other modes combined by 10×.
⚖️ The Volume–Value Paradox
~10%
Despite 92% volume share, UPI accounts for only ~10% of total transaction value. RTGS — with just 0.13% of volume — handles 71.4% of all money transferred. UPI dominates retail micro-payments; RTGS and NEFT remain the backbone of high-value institutional settlement.
📉 Debit Cards: A Dying Payment Mode
−44.6%
Debit card volumes collapsed from 1,768 lakh (Oct 2024) to just 979 lakh (Apr 2026) — a 44.6% fall in 19 months. UPI is directly cannibalising debit card usage. Banks should reconsider debit infrastructure investment and pivot resources to UPI-native products.
💳 Credit-on-UPI: The Next Battleground
+74%
Credit card volumes surged 74% from 3,215 lakh (Oct 2024) to 5,596 lakh (Apr 2026). Combined with NPCI's push for credit lines on UPI, this signals a powerful convergence. The fintech opportunity lies in BNPL and credit-on-UPI products embedded within everyday UPI flows.

📋 Data Source & Methodology
Transparency note

Data Source: Reserve Bank of India — Payment System Indicators (monthly public releases, Oct 2024 – Apr 2026)
Coverage: 6 payment modes · 19 consecutive months · 114 data points
Metrics: Volume (Lakh transactions) and Value (₹ Crore) for UPI, NEFT, RTGS, IMPS, Credit Cards, Debit Cards
Tools: Python (pandas) for data extraction & cleaning from RBI Excel files · Chart.js for interactive visualization
Data Quality Note: Certain months (Nov-25, Feb-26) show carry-forward values in RBI source files — flagged as a data quality observation consistent with the published indicators.
